📚 Research Publication & Project Background

This platform was researched and engineered by Neha H S during her undergraduate program at Global Academy of Technology. The motivation stems from the cognitive load students face when interpreting dynamic pointers, recursive stack memory, and relaxation steps in classical data structures and graph theory.
